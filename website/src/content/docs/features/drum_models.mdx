Sample-free 808 / 909-style drums as an oscillator type, ported from the drum engines of
Mutable Instruments' Plaits.

## Description

The **Drum** oscillator type plays one of five analog drum models instead of a waveform or a
sample:

| Model | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| 808 Kick | Bridged-T resonator kick with an attack pulse, self-FM and overdrive |
| 808 Snare | Two resonant shell modes (more at higher Tone) plus band-passed noise |
| Hi-hat | Six square oscillators mixed into metallic noise, band-passed and high-passed |
| 909 Kick | FM sine kick with a click and attack noise |
| 909 Snare | A pair of detuned sines with coupled resets plus filtered noise |

It works in kits (one model per row) and in synth clips, where a kick or snare can be played
chromatically. Pitch follows the note; the kick models play two octaves below it and the snares
one octave below, so a kit row at its default note lands in the classic register.

Each model has three macro controls, found in the oscillator's menu when its type is `Drum`:

- **Tone** – brightness / timbre (Plaits' TIMBRE).
- **Decay** – decay time (Plaits' MORPH).
- **Drive / Snappy / Noise** – the model-specific third control (Plaits' HARMONICS): attack FM
  and overdrive on the kicks, noise-vs-shell balance on the snares, clocked-noise amount on the
  hi-hat.

They live on the oscillator's pulse width, wave-index and carrier feedback parameters, so they
can be automated, patched from any modulation source and reached from the usual shortcut pads.

Velocity drives the models' **accent** input (harder hits are brighter and louder, not just
louder). A drum source behaves like a one-shot sample: note-offs are ignored and the voice ends
by itself once the drum has decayed, so no release time needs setting. The Deluge's envelopes,
filters and effects still apply on top.

## Enabling

Turn on `Drum Models` in the community features menu (`SETTINGS > COMMUNITY FEATURES`) to make
`Drum` selectable as an oscillator type. Presets and songs that already use it load and play
regardless of the setting.

## Example kits

`contrib/plaits_drums/` in the firmware repository contains two ready-made kits, `808 Models`
and `909 Models`, built entirely from these models - copy them into the `KITS` folder of your
SD card.
