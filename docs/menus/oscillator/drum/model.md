Selects which analog drum model this oscillator plays. The models are ports of the drum engines from Mutable Instruments' Plaits:

- **808 Kick** — bridged-T resonator kick with a punchy attack pulse and self-FM, plus overdrive at high *Drive*.
- **808 Snare** — two (or, at higher *Tone*, more) resonant shell modes plus band-passed noise.
- **Hi-hat** — six square oscillators mixed into "metallic" noise, band-passed and high-passed, with optional clocked noise.
- **909 Kick** — FM sine kick with a click and attack noise.
- **909 Snare** — pair of detuned sines with coupled resets plus filtered noise.
- **Hi-hat 2** — three ring-modulated square×saw oscillator pairs into a band-pass, with a two-stage (open/choke) envelope; more metallic and cymbal-like than the 808 hat.

Pitch follows the note as usual; the kick models play two octaves below the note and the snares one octave below, so a kit row at its default note lands in the classic register. Velocity drives the model's accent input.

A drum model behaves like a one-shot sample: note-offs are ignored and the voice ends by itself once the drum has decayed, so Envelope 1's release does not need to be set. The envelopes still shape the sound on top of the model's own decay.
