The model-specific third macro (Plaits' HARMONICS). Shown as **Drive** on the kick models (attack FM plus overdrive on the 808, FM envelope amount and decay on the 909), **Snappy** on the snares (noise vs. shell balance) and **Noise** on the hi-hat (amount of clocked noise mixed with the metallic oscillators).

Shares its underlying parameter with the FM carrier feedback, so anything patched or automated to feedback modulates this control.
